# Videos

`modules-cs.webp` are one silent animation of 29.5 seconds in two languages. It
explains what a module is, and its poster is the frame at 9.4 seconds. Lazurio shows the same files as
`modules-overview-en` and `modules-overview-cs`.

  `modules-en`, rendered on 2026-11-06 from revision

  screenshots of a product.
- **Content:** the fictional bakery Pekárna Kvásek and its fictional people and
  orders. No real account, person or customer appears.
- **Format:** 1920 × 1080, H.264, no sound track, `moov` before `mdat`.
