This course takes you from your first sign-in to an Environment to your
first task that an agent saves and you review. It is written for people
who are new in an Organization and work in a Work Environment. Each lesson
ends with one practical task and tells you how to know that it is done.

## Before you start

You need:

- **An Environment assigned to you** and its address. Your Organization
  assigns it; if you do not have the address, ask your Organization Admin.
- **Tailscale on an approved device**, if your Organization's Environments
  are reachable only in its tailnet. The tailnet is the default; see
  [Reach an Environment through Tailscale](/en/guide/tailscale/).
- **A ChatGPT or Claude account**, unless your Organization provides AI for
  its Environments.
- **A GitHub account** with access to your Organization's repositories.

## The lessons

1. [Sign in to your Environment](/en/guide/course/first-sign-in/): what an Environment is and which kind you work in.
2. [Finish Getting started](/en/guide/course/getting-started/): connect AI and GitHub and download your Organization.
3. [Find your way around](/en/guide/course/find-your-way/): Chat, Apps, Automate and Settings.
4. [Who works with you](/en/guide/course/who-works-with-you/): you, agents, colleagues, Henry and Buddy.
5. [Where things belong](/en/guide/course/where-things-belong/): Knowledgebase, Mission Control, an issue or Files.
6. [Connected apps](/en/guide/course/connected-apps/): what agents may do with your mail or calendar.
7. [Your first task](/en/guide/course/first-task/): brief an agent and have the result saved as a draft.
8. [Review and Publication](/en/guide/course/review/): check the result and decide who publishes it.
9. [When something does not work](/en/guide/course/when-stuck/): where to turn and what never to send.

You do lessons 1 and 2 once per Environment. Lessons 5 to 8 are one piece
of work: you choose it in lesson 5, have it saved in lesson 7 and review it
in lesson 8. Use real but non-sensitive work.

## What this course describes

The course describes Lazurio Platform as its public source stood on
1 November 2026. A newer release may show other labels. If your
Organization still works with the Lazurio Root on your own computer (GEN3),
some screens and steps differ.

The prompts are examples: copy them into Chat and adapt them to your work.
A prompt grants no access. Agents can use only what is signed in to your
Environment.

## After the course

- Save a recurring procedure and use it again: exercises 2 and 3 of
  [First steps with an agent](/en/guide/first-steps/).
- Let an agent work on a website: [Tips for working](/en/guide/work-tips/).
- Connect your mail or calendar: [Connect your tools](/en/guide/tool-connections/).
- Look up a term: [Glossary](/en/guide/glossary/).
