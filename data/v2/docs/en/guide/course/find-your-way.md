**In this lesson:** the three parts of Lazurio, where your Organization's
Modules are and how to get a page explained.

At the top of the left column is the switch **Chat · Apps · Automate**:

- **Chat** is where you work with agents. In an Environment it is T3 Code,
  labelled T3 Code (Lazurio). Start a new thread for each new task.
- **Apps** shows your Organization's Modules and the pages **Files**,
  **Integrations** and, where the Environment has a vault, **Vault**.
- **Automate** is for regular tasks and automation. Where it does not run,
  the tour says so.

A **Module** is one area of your work, for
example Mission Control for plans or a Knowledgebase for shared knowledge.
Under **Workspace**, Apps lists every Module of the Organization that your
Environment's GitHub identity can reach, each once. Under **Productionspace**
it shows the Organization's production repositories, read-only. Your
Organization decides which Modules it has. If a colleague mentions a Module
you do not see, you may not have access to it; ask your Organization Admin.
[Modules](/en/guide/modules/) explains what a module holds, how it comes to be
and who gets it.

**Files** is the Documents folder of this Environment. Upload materials for
agents there and download what they prepare, a whole folder as a ZIP if you
need it. The **?** after a page heading explains that page in place.

## Your task

1. In **Apps**, find one Module related to your work. Open its menu and
   choose **Module information**.
2. Open **Files** and look at what is there.
3. Open **Chat**, start a new thread and send:

   ```text
   I am new in this Organization. List the Modules I can see in Apps and
   say in one sentence what each one is for. Use only our Organization's
   own documents and say where you found each answer. Do not change anything.
   ```

**Done means:** you know which Module holds your Organization's plans and
which holds its shared knowledge, or that it has neither, and you can find
Files and Settings → Getting started again.
