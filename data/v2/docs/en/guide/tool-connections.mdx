## Before you start

The example takes you from connecting through verification to a first task. Have your AI app name, plan and intended account ready. Start with test or non-sensitive data.

**Follow your organization's rules in workplace Lazurio.** Its standard favors approved local MCP or CLI integrations; Composio and built-in connections are also explained here for standalone AI apps. This does not approve a new provider for company data.

## If the connection fails

Check the account, plan availability, administrator restrictions and authentication state. A prompt cannot supply a missing plugin or MCP server. Never paste passwords, tokens or sensitive diagnostic output into chat.

## Revoke access

Disconnect the account in the chosen integration service or AI app and check third-party permissions in your Google account too. For MCP, disable the server in your client and revoke its provider access according to the authentication method. Closing a chat does not revoke permissions.

This is a prompt and decision walkthrough, not a verified installation recipe for every client and plan combination. Verify specific screens and available operations during the real connection.
