## Before you start

Have your AI app name, plan and intended account ready. Start with test
or non-sensitive data.

**Follow your Organization's rules in Lazurio.** Choose new connections
using the [connected-app standard](/en/environment-app-connections/).
Composio is an option under that standard's conditions. Built-in connectors
are also shown for standalone AI apps; this example does not approve a new
provider for work data.

## If the connection fails

Check the account, plan availability, administrator restrictions and authentication state. A prompt cannot supply a missing connection or MCP server. Never paste passwords, tokens or sensitive diagnostic output into chat.

## Revoke access

Disconnect the account in the chosen integration service or AI app and check third-party permissions in your Google account too. For a Lazurio MCP connection, remove the server in Executor and revoke its provider access according to the authentication method. Closing a chat does not revoke permissions.

## Sources and example scope

Use this example to request a connection and check that it works. Exact setup steps depend on your AI app and plan; check that app's documentation.

- [Composio: authentication and connected accounts](https://docs.composio.dev/docs/authentication)
- [MCP: connect local servers](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/develop/connect-local-servers)
