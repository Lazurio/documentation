Want to ask Chat for tomorrow’s meetings? Connect the Google account that holds your work calendar in Lazurio. If you also want the agent to work with email, you can follow the same steps for Gmail.

## Before you start

Open the Environment where you want to use the calendar. In a Work or Team Environment, follow your Organization’s rules. If you share the Environment, check which team account to use.

## Connect Google Calendar

1. In **Apps → Integrations**, find **Google Calendar**. Its card shows how it connects in this Environment. You can also ask the agent for a link to the card.
2. If an account is already connected, check its email address. If yours is not connected yet, choose **Connect**. If the app uses Composio, Lazurio may ask you to sign in to Composio first.
3. On Google’s sign-in page, choose the correct account and read the requested permissions. If they are broader than you want to grant, stop here. After connecting, return to the card and check the account address.

[See the steps with screenshots of the app card](/en/environment-app-connections/#connecting-an-app).

## Check the connection against a meeting you know

Pick a day when your primary calendar has an event you recognize. For example, try this in Chat:

> Find tomorrow’s first meeting in my primary Google Calendar. Give me its title, date, time and time zone. If you can’t verify the connected account, stop. Leave the calendar unchanged and don’t send any invitations.

Compare the result with the event in the same Google account. This checks whether the agent can read the calendar. If you later ask it to send an invitation, review the time and recipients before sending.

## If the connection fails

If the agent finds no events, check the day, time zone and whether the event is on your primary calendar. If the agent reports that it lacks access, open the app card and check which account is connected. If the card has no option to connect, ask your Organization Admin for help. Keep passwords and sign-in credentials out of Chat.

## Revoke access

Open the app card, find the account and choose **Disconnect** if available. To withdraw consent at Google as well, open the third-party connections for that Google account and check which app you are removing. Environments using the same Composio account and Composio organization can share a connection. Check with your colleagues before disconnecting a shared account. [Manage third-party connections in Google](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/13533235).

## Sources and example scope

In a standalone AI app, check its documentation and plan for the available setup options. To revoke access for a custom MCP server, disable it where it is managed (in Executor in Lazurio) and revoke its provider permissions according to the sign-in method.
