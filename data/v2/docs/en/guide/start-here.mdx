Imagine a new personal assistant arriving for their first day. They can write,
research and prepare materials. But they have come from outside your company:
they do not know your routines, abbreviations or what good work looks like to you.

An agent needs a similar introduction: what to do, which materials to use
and how you will judge the result. You do not need a special formula.
Describe the task in your own words and correct misunderstandings. Lazurio
provides shared materials and rules for this work.

The human analogy helps with delegation; it is not literal. An agent is not
a person, can make mistakes and does not automatically have your experience
or access to everything. Here we mean an **agent working on a specific task**,
not automatically a long-term personal Buddy or a separate AI Colleague.

## What would you tell a new assistant?

Instead of “Prepare the meeting”, explain what you need:

```text
I am preparing for a team meeting. Use the attached notes to create a
one-page brief: what is done, what is blocked and what we need to decide.
Keep it concise for colleagues who know the project. Do not invent missing
facts; mark them as questions. Prepare a draft only; do not send it anywhere.
```

<details class="lz-learning-chat"><summary>See an example assistant response</summary><p><strong>AI · illustrative response:</strong> I will prepare those three sections. I will turn a missing deadline into a question rather than guess a date. First, I need the notes to work from.</p><p><strong>You:</strong> Here are the notes. For each blocked item, add who could help us.</p></details>

## Knowledgebase is your shared notebook

You would give a new assistant a company handbook. In Lazurio,
**Knowledgebase** serves that purpose: a place for shared knowledge,
procedures and context. You do not have to explain everything from scratch
each time — provided it is recorded correctly, accessible and loaded by the
agent for the task.

**Chat is the working conversation. Knowledgebase holds what should last.**
“Remember this” alone is not evidence that a rule was saved for other people.
Ask to see the actual entry, its location and who can access it. Private
information does not belong in the shared notebook.

```text
Turn what we clarified into a proposed procedure for our organization's
Knowledgebase. First check whether a similar procedure already exists.
Show the proposed changes, where they belong and who has access.
Do not publish yet. If you cannot access Knowledgebase, tell me.
```

## Introduce your work through real tasks

You do not need to document the entire company first. Start with one small
task. Give specific feedback, such as “Include a deadline only when the
source confirms it”. If the rule will be useful again, prepare a Knowledgebase
entry. Here, onboarding means supplying context and procedures, not training
the underlying AI model.

Next time, refer to the approved procedure and explain only what has changed.
