These external applications are not part of Lazurio. We recommend them because
they can make it easier to submit work and keep track of available AI limits.
Install them only when they comply with your Organization's rules.

<div class="lz-guide-apps">
  <article class="lz-guide-app">
    <div class="lz-guide-app__visual"><img src="/guide-assets/wispr-flow.svg" alt="Wispr Flow" /></div>
    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">Voice input</p><h2>Wispr Flow</h2></div>
    <p>Dictation that works in text fields across applications. Speak naturally and Wispr Flow turns your speech into polished text.</p>
    <p><strong>Useful when:</strong> You want to dictate a longer prompt, email, or message quickly instead of typing everything.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://wisprflow.ai/downloads" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="wispr-flow">Download Wispr Flow →</a>
  </article>
  <article class="lz-guide-app">
    <div class="lz-guide-app__visual lz-guide-app__visual--icon"><img src="/guide-assets/codexbar.svg" alt="CodexBar" /></div>
    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">AI limit overview</p><h2>CodexBar</h2></div>
    <p>A free, open-source macOS application that shows AI service usage, available limits, and reset times in the menu bar.</p>
    <p><strong>Useful when:</strong> Before assigning a longer task to an AI Colleague, you want to see how much capacity remains and when the limit resets.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://codexbar.app/" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="codexbar">Open CodexBar →</a>
  </article>
  <article class="lz-guide-app">
    <div class="lz-guide-app__visual lz-guide-app__visual--icon"><img src="/guide-assets/browser-use.svg" alt="Browser Use" /></div>
    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">AI work on the web</p><h2>Browser Use</h2></div>
    <p>A tool that lets an AI Colleague open a web page, click, fill in forms, and work with web applications according to your request.</p>
    <p><strong>Useful when:</strong> You want an AI Colleague to research information, work through an admin interface, or prepare a form without being told every individual step.</p>
    <p><strong>Keep in mind:</strong> Signing in does not increase its permissions. Sensitive, destructive, and publishing steps still require your confirmation.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://browser-use.com/" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="browser-use">Open Browser Use →</a>
  </article>
