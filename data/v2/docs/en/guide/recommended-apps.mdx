These apps can help you work with AI and prepare your own design system.
Check your organization's rules before using them with work data.

    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">Voice input</p><h2>Wispr Flow</h2></div>
    <p>Dictation that works in text fields across applications. Speak naturally and Wispr Flow turns your speech into polished text.</p>
    <p><strong>Useful when:</strong> You want to dictate a longer prompt, email, or message quickly instead of typing everything.</p>
    <p><strong>Privacy:</strong> With Context Awareness enabled, Flow can read surrounding text and conversation history. Its current documentation says it does not take screenshots. Before using work data, get its processing and permissions approved; in sensitive environments, turn off Context awareness in Settings → Data and Privacy. Check retention and model-training settings separately. <a href="https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/4678293671-Context-Awareness">What Wispr can read</a>.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://wisprflow.ai/downloads" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="wispr-flow">Download Wispr Flow →</a>

    <div class="lz-guide-app__visual lz-guide-app__visual--icon"><img src="/guide-assets/codexbar.svg" alt="CodexBar" /></div>
    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">AI limit overview</p><h2>CodexBar</h2></div>
    <p>A free, open-source macOS application that shows AI service usage, available limits, and reset times in the menu bar.</p>
    <p><strong>Useful when:</strong> Before assigning a longer task to an agent, you want to see how much capacity remains and when the limit resets.</p>
    <p><strong>Permissions:</strong> Depending on the service, CodexBar may use browser cookies, Keychain or OAuth credentials. Reading Safari cookies may require Full Disk Access. These permissions do more than display a number: get the specific access approved before connecting a work account and choose the least access needed. Never paste cookies or tokens into chat. <a href="https://github.com/steipete/CodexBar/blob/main/README.md#macos-permissions-why-theyre-needed">Permission documentation</a>.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://codexbar.app/" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="codexbar">Open CodexBar →</a>

    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">A Mac that stays awake</p><h2>Amphetamine</h2></div>
    <p>A free macOS app from the Mac App Store that keeps the computer from sleeping. When connected to power, it can keep a MacBook running even with the lid closed.</p>
    <p><strong>When it helps:</strong> When an agent is working on a longer task and you close the laptop or step away; the work does not fall asleep in the meantime.</p>
    <a class="lz-guide-app__link" href="https://apps.apple.com/app/amphetamine/id937984704" data-analytics-event="Guide App Click" data-analytics-app="amphetamine">Open Amphetamine in the App Store →</a>

    <div class="lz-guide-app__visual"><img src="/guide-assets/swatchfin.svg" alt="Swatchfin" /></div>
    <div><p class="lz-guide-app__eyebrow">Creating a design system</p><h2>Swatchfin</h2></div>
    <p>Swatchfin collects your logo, colours, fonts and tone of voice from your website. Use it when starting a design system that builds on the brand you already have.</p>
    <p><strong>Getting started:</strong> Enter your public website URL. Review the result and give it to your agent as material for a design system module in Lazurio. Colours and fonts can be exported as CSS or named values for use in applications.</p>
    <p>Swatchfin is open source under the MIT licence. It gives you a starting point; add rules, components and examples in your own design system.</p>
    <p><a href="https://github.com/ankanhq/swatchfin">Source code and instructions on GitHub</a></p>
