**Not only its code.** Its projects, tickets, TODOs, decisions, documentation,
approvals—and the evidence of who changed what and why.

Surprisingly, much of a company's work can live there. Not its bank account,
payroll or every SaaS application. But the part that benefits from clear
ownership, version history, scoped access, review and deliberate publication
can.

Lazurio is a framework for translating that part of a company into private,
GitHub-native processes. Repositories hold the work. GitHub permissions define
the boundaries. People set the direction and retain authority. Task Agents
coordinate inside those boundaries, turning human intent into Drafts, checks
and evidence that people can review before Publication.

Lazurio does not ask you to hand your company over to AI. It gives people and
Agents the same operating context: visible, versioned and reviewable.
Agents can move work forward, but they do not choose the company's direction or
gain authority from a prompt.

## What Lazurio is today

Today Lazurio runs from a public source checkout using Git and Bun. It includes
Launchpad, diagnostics, an experimental CLI v0 and the operating contracts used
by connected Organizations and modules. A simple packaged installation is a
target, not a released distribution.

It is not an AI model, a managed SaaS tenant or a universal security sandbox
around the agent client. Its value is narrower and more practical: it makes
authority, context, work and publication boundaries visible enough to govern.
The [security and control evidence](/en/public-evidence/) shows where those claims
come from and what they do not prove.
