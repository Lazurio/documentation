In Lazurio, people work with AI agents using shared materials. Procedures,
plans and applications stay where the team can find them and review changes.

It builds on GitHub, which can hold tasks, documentation and decisions as well
as code. History shows who changed what; permissions control who can read, edit
and approve the work. You do not need to move accounting, email or
every other existing service into GitHub.

The agent works with the materials and tools available for the task. It
prepares a draft, checks it and presents it for review. You define the goal;
an authorized person decides whether to publish or deploy the result.

## What Lazurio is today

Lazurio Platform v0.1.14 is an installable release, published on 27 October
2026. It includes the command-line tool, Apps and diagnostics. It also prepares
the instructions agents read in your Lazurio Folder. You do not need a checkout
of the Platform source code for everyday work. See [deployment and
updates](/en/deployment-operations/).

You choose the AI client and model provider separately. A self-hosted
installation does not require Lazurio's optional account and managed-hosting
services. Lazurio does not add a universal security sandbox around AI tools;
check the providers and access used by your deployment.
The [security and control evidence](/en/public-evidence/) explains these boundaries.
