Lazurio is a working environment for people and AI agents. It keeps company
procedures, plans and applications where the team can find them and review
changes.

It builds on GitHub, which can hold tasks, documentation and decisions as well
as code. Version history shows who changed what. Permissions control who can
read, edit and approve the work. You do not need to move accounting, email or
every other existing service into GitHub.

The agent works with the materials and tools available for the task. It
prepares a draft, checks it and presents it for review. You define the goal;
an authorized person decides whether to publish or deploy the result.

## What Lazurio is today

The version described in the linked sources runs from source with Git and Bun. It includes
Launchpad, diagnostics, an experimental CLI v0 and the operating contracts used
by connected Organizations and modules. A simple packaged installation is a
target, not a released distribution.

You choose the AI model and client separately. Lazurio itself is not a
managed SaaS service or a universal security sandbox for AI tools. Review
the selected providers' terms before deployment.
The [security and control evidence](/en/public-evidence/) shows where those claims
come from and what they do not prove.
