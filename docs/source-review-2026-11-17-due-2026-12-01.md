# Source verification — 17 November 2026, sources due on 1 December 2026

Seventy-two sources were due for review on 1 December 2026. Each one was opened
again on 17 November 2026 and compared with the claims of every page in both
locales that cites it. A review window moves only where the source still
supports those claims, or where the page was corrected first. All seventy-two
windows move: 48 sources still support their pages as written, and 24 needed a
correction of the page text first, three of them after decisions that Matěj
Suchánek made on 17 November 2026 (see [Owner's decisions](#owners-decisions)).
No source was unavailable and none stays expired. Six sources are new. The
repository's monthly cadence is retained: a renewed or new source is next due
one calendar month after this review, on 17 December 2026. The nearest
remaining due date in the map is 2 December 2026 (three sources).

This record is the second source review of 17 November 2026; the first, for

## Owner's decisions

Matěj Suchánek decided four points on 17 November 2026. They were relayed in
the working thread of this review and are recorded here as his decisions, not
as provider or Lazurio source evidence.

- **"Assignment required" is a required step of the Microsoft 365 company
  app.** Step 6 of the Organization Admin's procedure on `microsoft-365` is no
  longer a recommendation. With assignment required, Microsoft always asks for
  administrator consent, so the sentence that a new permission makes the
  sign-in report that administrator approval is required now holds as written
  and stays unchanged. Step 6 adds that assigning a group needs Microsoft Entra
  ID P1 or P2, and that without it users are assigned one by one; step 8 reads
  the assignment back. The GEN3 runbooks still call the step recommended, and
  LazurioPlatform's company app section does not mention it.
- **Redirect URIs and "Allow public client flows" stay as written.** Microsoft's
  redirect URI page says that an app with redirect URIs needs a public client
  flow configuration, while step 3 tells IT to leave the switch off. The text
  stays, and `issue-2026-11-17-002` records the open check: at the first pilot,
  verify the code exchange without a secret on an HTTPS return address with the
  switch off.
- **LinkedIn: the documentation promises only the official API.** Lazurio
  connects LinkedIn only through its official API, through Composio, and an
  agent may do what that API allows. Agents never automate LinkedIn's website,
  no scraping and no bots, and read it only under the user's supervision.
  `provider-notes` follows this in both locales and says that supervised
  reading is Lazurio's own rule, not an exception in LinkedIn's agreement.
- **The English interface still says "Launchpad".** Renaming it in the product
  is tracked in LazurioPlatform. Meanwhile the English glossary says that the
  home page of Apps and the browser tab still show "Launchpad" in release
  v0.1.21.

  `Calendars.ReadWrite`, `Files.ReadWrite.All` and `Sites.Read.All`, and all
  nine permission descriptions are unchanged. Delegated `Mail.Send` does not
  need administrator consent, which made the sentence on new permissions too

  decision.

  roles that grant tenant-wide consent, app registration by any user or the
  Application Developer role, the registration path and "Single tenant only",
  the "Mobile and desktop applications" platform, "Assignment required" and
  "Enabled for users to sign in?", 256 redirect URIs and the ignored
