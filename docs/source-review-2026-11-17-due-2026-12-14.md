## Owner's decision

- **People sign in to Organization tailnets with their Lazurio account today
  (`issue-2026-11-17-005`, source `lazurio-decision-register-access-0245-0247`).**
  Decision 0246 says under "What runs today", identically at the source's old
  pin, in v0.1.22 and on main: "Until Lazurio App is released, people enter as
  before, through the Tailscale app and the Environment's web interface ... No
  tailnet uses this sign-in with the Lazurio account yet".
  `guide/tailscale-add-device` and `guide/device-approval` tell people to sign
  in with their Lazurio account and to expect "Node registered", as the agent
  manual at v0.1.22 (`src/folder/manual.ts`, Adding a computer to a tailnet,
  step 4) does. On 18 November 2026, at about 23:00 UTC, Matěj Suchánek
  answered in the working thread of this review, choosing the first option:
  people already sign in to their Organization's tailnet with their Lazurio
  account. The guides therefore describe the procedure that runs today and stay
  unchanged. The sentence in 0246 is out of date; correcting it is tracked in
  [LazurioPlatform#1086](https://github.com/Lazurio/LazurioPlatform/issues/1086),
  which links the register line at `0eb2e3a`. The answer is recorded here as
  the owner's decision, not as provider or Lazurio source evidence. The source
  is re-pinned to main at `0eb2e3a`, its title says the same, and it is renewed
  on 18 November 2026 until 18 December 2026. `issue-2026-11-17-005` moved to
  `ISSUES.resolved.json`.

- **Wispr Flow.** The downloads page still says that Flow for desktop "runs in

  (macOS 14 or later) that tracks usage windows, credit balances and reset

  Closed-Display Mode session offers Power Protect, a separate download for
  changes of the power source. The guide said the lid-closed mode works "when
  connected to power" and now says what the store says, in both locales.

## Lazurio sources

- **Decision 0243.** At the source's pin (`c6c320b`, 12 November 2026) 0243
  held the rule that every Environment is entered only through a tailnet. By
  14 November it was superseded by 0245, 0246 and 0247, and on main only its
  heading and status line remain ("superseded by 0245, 0246 and 0247"). No page
  has cited it since the pages moved to 0245 to 0247 (commit `413d7bf`); the
  rule it held is cited through `lazurio-decision-register-access-0245-0247`
  and `lazurio-decision-register-network-0246`. Replacing it with another pin of
  0246 would duplicate those sources, so it is removed.
- **Decisions 0245 to 0247 and 0234.** The source's pin `25c72bc` and release
  v0.1.22 carry the same text of 0245, 0246 and 0247. On main, 0245 gained an
  amendment of 17 November 2026, decided and not yet deployed: the
  Organization's own servers decide entry by the boundaries that Machines
  applied, a change of the boundaries is approved only by an Owner in a way
  Lazurio cannot imitate (today the merge on GitHub), an Admin approves a device
  through the service on the Conglomerate Host with a step taken in person, an
  agent may prepare an approval but never give it, a person's further devices
  are confirmed from an approved one, and the service's audit entry is the one
  GEN4 record of an approval. 0246 gained links between T3 Code servers along a
  Personal Environment's edge, and 0247 says that Auth's credential is
  necessary and never sufficient for entry. 0245 to 0247 are identical on main
  at `0eb2e3a`. Of 0234 the source covers only the interim rule of
  13 November 2026, which still stands: until the Organization Admin role is
  deployed, an active Organization Owner performs the Admin's administrative
  acts and gets no access to Environments by them; the rest of 0234 has its own
  source, `lazurio-decision-0234-role-names` (see
  [Found outside this review](#found-outside-this-review)). None of this
  contradicts the eighteen citing pages: they describe
  0245 to 0247 as the target model with the transition not complete, the
  Organization Owner performing the Admin's acts today without access to any
  Environment, the Admin's service entry through a recording entry point and
  observing by default, the host administered only by its holder, the tailnet
  as the only way in with two public entries still running, and the GEN4
  approval record as amended. The one conflict was the sign-in, settled by the
  owner's decision above.
